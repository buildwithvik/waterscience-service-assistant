## KB-02 Will CLEO fit my tap or shower?
- CLEO Shower & Tap filters fit **threaded metal** taps of **22 mm, 24 mm and 28 mm**, standard ½-inch shower arms, overhead showers and hand showers.
- **Does not fit plastic taps.**
- For taps, remove the aerator (the small mesh cap at the tap mouth) and fit the aerator adaptor from the kit.
- If unsure, the customer can send a clear photo of the tap mouth and the team will confirm.
- Shower-head filters (Compact, Multi-Flow, Rain) fit standard ½-inch shower arms.

## KB-03 How to install (tap)
1. Unscrew the aerator from the tap mouth.
2. Screw the aerator adaptor from the kit onto the tap.
3. Wrap the adaptor thread with Teflon tape. Make sure at least 2 washers are inside the top connector of the CLEO.
4. Screw the CLEO onto the adaptor, turning clockwise.
5. Run water for 3 minutes. Dark water at first is carbon dust and is harmless.
6. Tighten with the spanner. If it leaks, add a washer and tighten again.
- Installation video and help: WhatsApp the support team.

## KB-04 How to install (shower arm or hand shower)
1. Remove the shower head (or hose) and clean the thread.
2. Wrap the thread with Teflon tape. Put 2 washers in the CLEO top connector.
3. Screw the CLEO on clockwise.
4. Run water for 3 minutes to flush carbon dust.
5. Screw the shower head or hose onto the bottom of the CLEO, with a washer. Tighten with the spanner.
- No plumber needed; takes about 3–5 minutes.

## KB-05 Low water pressure right after installing
- CLEO comes with a water-saving mesh washer (flow restrictor) inside the top connector.
- If home water pressure is low, remove that mesh washer and use the plain washer from the kit. Flow improves.

## KB-06 Water flow reduced slowly over time / filter clogged
Likely reasons:
- Sediment has built up inside the cartridge (common with borewell or tanker water).
- The filter was not used for many days and sediment hardened into lumps.
- The mesh washer is blocked.
- The cartridge has reached the end of its life (about 25,000 litres).
Fix (reverse flush):
1. Open the filter by turning the bottom shell anti-clockwise. Take out the cartridge.
2. Tap the cartridge sides gently to loosen lumps.
3. Put it back **blue side up**, close, and run water for 3–5 minutes.
4. Open again, put the cartridge back **red side up**, and close.
5. Clean the mesh washer. If flow is still low, replace the cartridge.
- Note: a cartridge choked by sediment or long disuse is **not covered** by warranty (see KB-12).

## KB-07 Leaking
- Leak from the **middle joint** (where the two halves meet): the cartridge O-ring is out of place. Open, re-seat the O-ring, close firmly.
- Leak from the **top or bottom connector**: add 1–2 more washers, wrap Teflon tape on the tap/shower thread, tighten with the spanner.
- If the **body is cracked**, it is not a fitting issue: see KB-11.

## KB-08 Cartridge: when to replace and which one to buy
- Replace about every **6 months** (about 25,000 litres). Sooner in high-sediment areas.
- Two cartridge types:
  - **Metro/Municipal**: for city supply with chlorine and moderate hardness.
  - **Borewell/Tanker**: for high hardness with no chlorine.
- Cartridges are sold on the website and Amazon (CLEO cartridge pack of 2: ₹1,515 on website at time of check).
- If left unused for over 2 weeks, sediment can form lumps; flush or replace.

## KB-09 How to replace the cartridge
1. Hold the top shell, turn the bottom shell anti-clockwise to open.
2. Remove the old cartridge. Remove stickers from both sides of the new one.
3. Insert the new cartridge **red side up**.
4. Close by turning clockwise. Run water for 3 minutes.

## KB-10 "It does not reduce TDS" / "I see no difference"
- CLEO filters **do not reduce TDS**. TDS only shows something is dissolved, not whether it is harmful. CLEO changes hardness salts so they do not stick to hair, skin or tiles and wash away.
- CLEO does not remove iron.
- Lather and feel of water improve right away. Hair and skin changes usually take **4–6 weeks**.
- A simple "lather test" with soap shows the difference.
- Hot water: max **45°C**; run cold water first.
- **[CONFLICT]** Hot water limit is 45°C on the website, 60°C on an Amazon listing, 70°C in the old manual. Approved: 45°C (safest).

## KB-15 Which product should I buy?
- Taps and hand showers, city water: CLEO Shower & Tap Filter SFU-717 (Metro/Municipal cartridge).
- Taps, borewell or tanker water: CLEO Shower & Tap Filter for Borewell/Tanker.
- Overhead shower only: Compact Shower Filter or Multi-Flow Shower Filter.
- Whole house / geyser and appliance protection: Whole House Softener (needs consultation; book via website).
- Washing machine: Washing Machine Filter.
- Ask: water source (city or borewell/tanker), where it will be fitted (tap or shower), tap material (metal or plastic).
